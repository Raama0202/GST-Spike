# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The suite serves three related groups: platform operators who manage tenants and licenses, company owners and Chartered Accountants who reconcile GST activity, and field staff who capture purchase invoices. Users are usually in work mode, under deadline pressure, and need fast confidence that invoices, subscriptions, reminders, and sync status are correct.

## Product Purpose

GST SaaS Suite is an end-to-end operating system for GST invoice capture, tenant licensing, reconciliation, reminders, and reporting. Success means invoice data flows from client capture into company operations and platform administration without broken ownership, unclear state, or manual re-entry.

## Positioning

One connected GST operations platform where licensing, capture, reconciliation, and reminders share the same tenant-aware source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Premium, exacting, calm. The product should feel like financial-grade operational software: luxurious in polish, restrained in workflow, and clear under pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic bootstrap dashboards, decorative SaaS landing-page tropes, overanimated admin panels, and interfaces where Super Admin, Admin Portal, and Client Capture feel like unrelated products.

## Design Principles

- Cross-product continuity first: every license, company, invoice, reminder, and feature flag should visibly connect across the three products.
- Dense where it helps, spacious where decisions matter.
- State is a first-class material: sync, OCR confidence, subscription status, reconciliation status, and audit history must always be legible.
- Premium polish supports trust, never spectacle.
- Offline and failure paths must feel expected, not exceptional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, responsive layouts, clear focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated transitions.
